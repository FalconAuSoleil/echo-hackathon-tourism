Inside the phone

Seven seconds of German, in six steps.

  1. ListenWhisper transcribes and detects the language.
  2. Forget the voiceThe audio is deleted, the buffer zero-filled.
  3. Remove who said itNames, phone numbers, e-mails, @handles: scrubbed before anything is stored.
  4. Cut into ideasSentences, then clauses: "but", "mais", "aber", "pero"…
  5. Match the meaningEach idea is compared with 21 known findings, in any of 4 languages. Counted only if confident and the negation agrees.
  6. Keep only the countThe words of a counted remark are not kept. Just +1.
