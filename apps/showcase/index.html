What it takes

A phone the family already has.
A little signal, now and then.

One entry-level Android Her daughter's smartphone is enough. Echo is installed on it once.
A bit of signal Only so WhatsApp can bring the voice notes in. A few minutes of 3G is plenty.
✈
Then, airplane mode is fine Listening, understanding, counting: all of it happens on the phone. No computer, no Wi-Fi, no account.

No developer, no subscription, no server. Noor's household can run it on its own.

Inside the phone

What Echo does with Lena's seven seconds.

  1. It listensThe voice becomes text, whatever the visitor's language.
  2. It forgets who spokeThe recording is deleted. Names and phone numbers are removed.
  3. It finds the ideasOne message can hold a compliment and a complaint. Echo reads them apart.
  4. It keeps only a tallyNot her words. Just one more visitor who liked this, one more who struggled with that.
